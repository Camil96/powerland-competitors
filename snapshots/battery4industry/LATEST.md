CONTACT US The applications of a battery system with smart control A data-driven Energy Management System (EMS) is crucial to ensure your investment in battery storage delivers optimal returns. We collaborate with EMS providers that grant access to the most innovative revenue models and ensure flexibility for a range of applications and energy suppliers. Below is a brief overview of some key applications of a battery system: Afbeelding Self-Consumption Store surplus energy from your PV system to use later and maximize the return on your solar investment. Afbeelding Peak Shaving Reduce your power demand and minimize the risk of being charged the capacity tariff by flattening peaks. Make full use of your grid connection as an alternative to grid reinforcement. Afbeelding Day-Ahead Energy Trading Take advantage of the volatility in the variable energy market, purchasing energy (kWh) during low-price hours to use later. Afbeelding Imbalance Energy Trading Capitalize on the fluctuations in the imbalance market, buying and selling energy (kWh) at highly volatile prices. Afbeelding Grid Services Provide flexible power (kW) to Elia, the high-voltage grid operator, in exchange for a compensation.
